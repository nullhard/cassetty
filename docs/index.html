cassetty

A cassette deck music player for your terminal.

macOS & Linux · keyboard & mouse · MP3 FLAC WAV OGG, more with ffmpeg

SKIN or press t

LATEST all releases & checksums

any Mac or Linux box
$ curl -fsSL https://github.com/nullhard/cassetty/releases/latest/download/cassetty_$(uname -s | tr A-Z a-z)_$(uname -m | sed 's/x86_64/amd64/;s/aarch64/arm64/').tar.gz | tar xz cassetty
$ sudo mv cassetty /usr/local/bin/
$ cassetty ~/Music
with Go 1.26+
$ go install github.com/nullhard/cassetty@latest
  • macOS: if you downloaded the archive with a browser, clear the quarantine flag once: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine cassetty
  • Linux: plays through PulseAudio or PipeWire, falls back to ALSA.
  • More formats: with ffmpeg on your PATH cassetty also plays M4A/AAC/ALAC, Opus, AIFF, WMA, APE and more.
